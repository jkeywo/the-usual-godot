# Asset provenance

`original/*.svg`: original geometric pixel artwork authored for this port,
released under this repository's MIT license. No source imagery from the
restricted CozyHome or character packs was used or copied. Source SVGs use
integer-aligned rectangles and nearest-neighbour rendering.

`kenney_future_narrow.ttf`, `click-a.ogg`, `tap-a.ogg`: Kenney UI Pack, CC0,
retained from the baseline repository's documented curated subset.
https://kenney.nl/assets/ui-pack

The original repository's restricted sprites and raw asset packs are excluded
from this repository and its exports.

`original/resident_*_{walk0,walk1,work0,work1,sit,sleep,blink}.svg` and
`original/audio/*.wav`: original posed artwork and synthesized PCM cues,
reproducibly generated by `tools/generate_presentation.py`. MIT licensed with
this project. No recordings, samples, melodies or third-party sprite frames
were used. Room/outdoor tones are quiet loops; cue playback starts only after
user interaction and can be muted.

`characters/*.svg`: original integer-grid vector pixel artwork and modular
animation atlases authored for this project, generated reproducibly by
`tools/generate_characters.py`; released under the repository MIT license.
These contain no third-party sprite data or raster samples. The AI-generated
boards in `docs/art/` are exploratory references only and are excluded from
exports. Runtime atlases have independent head layers and shared clothing/body
geometry, with hand colours generated per skin palette. Four residents and five
outfits each support eight directions, eight walk frames, six talking gestures
and six interaction poses, plus idle, seated and sleeping states.
